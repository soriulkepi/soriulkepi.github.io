Come ask answerable questions about health

We look for curious, rigorous people who want to understand how genes and environment shape health. A background in medicine, public health, statistics, biology, or data science is welcome — and so is a willingness to learn.

유전과 환경이 건강을 어떻게 형성하는지 탐구하고 싶은 분을 찾습니다. 의학·보건학·통계·생명·데이터과학 배경을 환영하며, 배우려는 의지를 가장 중요하게 봅니다.

What you will get here

우리 연구실에 오면

  • Real cohort data. Hands-on work with KoGES and multi-national sleep genomic cohorts — not simulated exercises. 실제 대규모 코호트 데이터로 학습·연구
  • Method training across fields. Epidemiologic design, longitudinal and survival analysis, genomic analysis, and machine learning on physiological signals. 역학설계·종단분석·유전체분석·머신러닝 훈련
  • International collaboration. Opportunities to join ongoing work with the University of Pennsylvania and international consortia. 국제 공동연구 참여 기회
  • Publication from the start. Students are supported toward first-author papers, conference presentations, and grant experience. 제1저자 논문·학회 발표·과제 참여 지원
