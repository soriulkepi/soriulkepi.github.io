연구실 구성원

Soriul Kim, Ph.D.

Principal Investigator · Assistant Professor

책임연구자 · 강원대학교 의과대학 예방의학교실 조교수

I am an epidemiologist working at the intersection of clinical and genetic epidemiology. My training began in clinical laboratory science, moved through epidemiology and biostatistics, and settled in molecular genetic and clinical epidemiology. I now study how genes, sleep, and environment jointly shape brain health and chronic disease across the life course.

임상병리에서 출발해 역학·보건통계를 거쳐 분자유전 및 임상역학으로 이어지는 훈련을 받았습니다. 현재는 유전·수면·환경이 생애 전반의 뇌건강과 만성질환을 어떻게 함께 형성하는지 연구합니다.

The most reliable evidence for prevention comes from following real populations over time and linking what we observe to its biological basis. My aim is to turn that evidence into practical prevention.
