Research

Four tracks, one question: who gets sick, and why

Our work sits where clinical epidemiology meets genetic epidemiology. We follow real populations over time, link what we observe to its genetic and physiological basis, and translate the result into prevention.

임상역학과 유전역학의 교차점에서, 인구집단을 시간에 걸쳐 추적하고 그 관찰을 유전적·생리적 기반과 연결해 예방으로 옮깁니다.

Ch 01 · Sleep & BrainSleep

Sleep and brain health

수면·뇌건강 코호트 역학

We examine how habitual sleep — apnea severity, duration, latency, chronotype, and the sleep EEG itself — relates to cognitive aging, brain structure, and long-term mortality. Repeated follow-up lets us treat sleep as both a marker and a modifiable risk factor.

폐쇄성수면무호흡, 수면시간·잠복기, 크로노타입, 수면뇌파가 인지노화·뇌구조 변화·장기 사망률과 어떻게 연관되는지 반복 추적자료로 규명합니다.

Ch 02 · GenomeGenes

Molecular genetic epidemiology

분자유전역학 · GWAS · 유전자–환경 상호작용

We integrate genome-wide association analysis, heritability estimation, and candidate genotypes with environmental and lifestyle exposures — asking not only which variants matter, but under what conditions they matter. Findings are followed through to functional validation where possible.

전장유전체연관분석·유전율 추정·후보유전형을 환경·생활습관 노출과 통합해, 어떤 변이가 어떤 조건에서 위험으로 작동하는지 규명하고 기능검증까지 연결합니다.

GWASheritabilitygene–environmentfunctional validation
Ch 03 · DiseaseRisk

Cardiometabolic and cerebrovascular disease

심뇌혈관·대사질환 종단분석

Using long-term prospective cohorts, we quantify incidence, trajectories, and mortality for atherosclerosis, diabetes, cerebral small vessel disease, and related conditions — focusing on early, modifiable predictors that precede clinical disease.

장기 전향적 코호트에서 죽상경화·당뇨·뇌소혈관질환 등의 발생과 변화 궤적, 사망률을 정량화하고 임상질환에 선행하는 조기·교정가능 인자를 탐색합니다.

atherosclerosisdiabetescerebral small vessel diseasemortality
Ch 04 · PredictionAI

Machine learning and precision prevention

AI 기반 유전체·뇌파 통합분석 · 정밀예방

We develop machine and deep learning methods that combine genomic data with physiological signals — most notably the sleep EEG — into biomarkers and risk-prediction models, then translate them toward clinical and community prevention.

유전체와 생리신호(특히 수면뇌파)를 결합한 머신러닝·딥러닝 바이오마커와 위험예측모형을 개발하고, 이를 임상·지역사회 예방으로 중개합니다.

Cohorts, data, and collaborations

활용 코호트 · 데이터 · 협력

Cohort

KoGES

한국인유전체역학조사 · 안산 코호트

A long-running prospective study of community-dwelling Korean adults, with repeated examinations including polysomnography, neuroimaging, and cognitive testing.

Consortium

SAGIC · SHIP

국제 수면·인구 코호트 컨소시엄

Multi-national sleep and population cohort platforms supporting cross-ethnic comparison of sleep phenotypes and genetic architecture.

Partners

International collaboration
