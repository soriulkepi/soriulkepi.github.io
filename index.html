Population · Multimodal Health Data · Prediction · Prevention

Advancing Precision Prevention through Integrative Population Health Research

We integrate genomics, medical imaging, sleep health, environmental exposures, and large-scale population cohorts to understand disease mechanisms, predict risk early, and generate evidence for precision prevention and healthy aging.

대규모 인구집단 코호트와 유전체, 의료영상, 수면, 환경 데이터를 통합하여 질병의 기전을 이해하고 위험을 조기에 예측하며, 건강한 노화와 정밀예방의학을 위한 근거를 만들어갑니다.

Ch 01 · Population

Population & Clinical Epidemiology

Large-scale prospective cohorts and real-world health data to identify population-level determinants of chronic disease and healthy aging.

  • Large-scale Population Cohorts · Real-world Health Data
  • Environmental Exposure · Sex Differences
 Ch 02 · Multimodal Health Data

Genomics, Imaging & Sleep Health

Integrating genomics, quantitative imaging, and sleep phenotyping to uncover the biological mechanisms underlying complex diseases.

  • GWAS & PRS · Gene-Environment Interaction
  • Imaging Biomarkers · Sleep Phenotyping
 Ch 03 · Prediction

Machine Learning & Risk Prediction

Machine learning, deep learning, and statistical models integrating multimodal data to improve disease prediction and precision prevention.

  • Machine & Deep Learning · Deep Phenotyping
  • Digital Biomarker · Risk Prediction Models
 Ch 04 · Prevention

Precision Prevention & Healthy Aging

Identifying modifiable determinants of chronic disease and healthy aging to enable early risk prediction and precision prevention.

  • Precision Prevention · Healthy Aging
  • Modifiable Risk Reduction · Healthspan Extension
